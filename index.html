♞ Choach

Find out why your mistakes are mistakes

Enter your Lichess username above. Choach downloads your recent games, runs Stockfish over them in your browser, and then does two things Lichess's own analysis doesn't:

  • Explains each mistake in words — which piece was left hanging and why, what fork or pin you walked into, what you could have won instead — with the punishing line and the better line to step through on the board.
  • Looks for patterns across all your games — the kinds of mistake you repeat, when they happen (rushed moves, time trouble, winning positions), and what to practise first.

Everything runs on your computer. Only your public games are fetched from Lichess; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Engine: Stockfish 19 lite (stockfish.js, GPL-3). Board: Lichess chessground. Game data: Lichess API.
